1
The Coordinator creates a room, enters each leader's name and march time, and shares their screen on Discord.
2
Rally Leaders can optionally join the same room code to see their personal cue on their own device — or just watch the coordinator's shared screen.
3
Coordinator hits GO — a 10-second countdown fires on every connected screen simultaneously.
4
Each leader's row shows their staggered cue with a draining progress bar. All rallies arrive at the castle at the same time.
